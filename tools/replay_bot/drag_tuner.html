replay-bot drag tuner

Turns each hardcoded timing in the seek-by-drag gesture into a slider, runs one real drag (or the four-target sweep) with the live values, and reports whether it landed and whether the HUD had settled by the time it was read. Costs no codes - needs a replay open with its controls up (press N).

Test calibrates this map's bar, drags to the target second, waits settle, then reads the playhead and the HUD's worst cell. A small error means the drag landed; a low worst cell (under ~0.6) means the portrait band was still drawing and settle is too short.
Sweep runs probe_drag's four targets (120/300/600/180s) the same way.
Save writes the sliders to drag_timing.json, which drag.js loads over its defaults - so run.js and the offline tests pick it up too. Once the numbers hold across a few replays, write them into drag.js's TIMING as the new defaults and delete the json.
